# Portraits synthétiques de démonstration

Les 16 portraits représentent exclusivement des adultes entièrement fictifs. Ils servent uniquement au jeu de données de démonstration SGP MAKALA. Aucune photographie de personne réelle n'a été utilisée comme référence.

Génération : outil intégré `image_gen.imagegen`, le 10 octobre 2026. L'atlas a été inspecté visuellement avant découpage mécanique en 16 JPEG de 300 × 300 pixels par Pillow. Le manifeste indique l'empreinte de l'atlas source et de chaque fichier.

Les lignes 1–2 de l'atlas contiennent les portraits `portrait_m_01.jpg` à `portrait_m_08.jpg`; les lignes 3–4 les portraits `portrait_f_01.jpg` à `portrait_f_08.jpg`.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: neutral fictional-person portrait atlas for a software demonstration dataset.
Primary request: Create ONE square image arranged as an exact 4-by-4 contact sheet of 16 distinct fictional adults, suitable for mechanically cutting the image into 16 square headshot assets.
Scene/backdrop: each tile uses the same plain light gray studio background; tiles fill the whole image with no outer margin and no gutters or borders.
Subjects: entirely fictional Central African adults whose appearance is plausible for residents of the Democratic Republic of Congo. All subjects are at least 20 years old. First two rows: eight distinct men of varied adult ages (approximately 22, 28, 34, 39, 44, 50, 57, 65). Last two rows: eight distinct women of varied adult ages (approximately 23, 29, 35, 40, 46, 51, 58, 64). Diverse natural hairstyles, face shapes, skin tones within dark African complexion range. Some men have neat short facial hair; all eyes clearly visible.
Style/medium: realistic studio headshot photography, natural skin detail, no glamour retouch.
Composition/framing: every equal square tile contains exactly one centered person's full head, hair, neck, and upper shoulders with comfortable breathing room. Direct front facing, eye line consistent across tiles, no heads crossing tile boundaries.
Lighting/mood: gentle even studio lighting, neutral expression, dignified and calm.
Clothing: plain unbranded civilian shirts and blouses in muted colors (navy, white, beige, charcoal, olive, dusty blue), no uniforms, no prison clothing, no institutional setting.
Text: none.
Constraints: exactly 16 clearly different faces, 4 rows and 4 columns; 8 men in the upper half and 8 women in the lower half. No real persons, no celebrities, no names, no numbers, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no accessories obscuring faces.
```
